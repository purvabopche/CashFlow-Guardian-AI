# CashFlow Guardian AI 📊
## 8-Slide Pitch Deck Presentation Outline

### Slide 1 — The Problem: Cash Flow Invisibility
- **Title**: The Silent Killer of Small Businesses
- **Main Content**:
  - 82% of small businesses and freelancers fail due to cash flow timing mismatches, not lack of profitability.
  - A company can have ₹100,000 in invoiced revenue but run out of cash before payroll next week.
  - Founders discover liquidity crunches 48 hours before an overdraft when it is too late to react.
- **Suggested Visual**:
  - Split graphic: *"Profitable on Paper"* (Green ledger) vs. *"Negative Bank Balance"* (Red bank alert).

### Slide 4 — How It Works: The Decision Loop

  - **4. Act**: Delivers 1-click remedies (automated reminders, bill postponement) to restore safety.
