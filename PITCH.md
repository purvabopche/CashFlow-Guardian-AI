# CashFlow Guardian AI 🎤
## 2-Minute Hackathon Pitch Script

*Estimated Speaking Time: 1 minute 50 seconds – 2 minutes*  
*Tone: Clear, confident, conversational, and product-focused.*

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] HOOK
> "Imagine opening your business bank account this morning. You see a comfortable balance, and everything looks fine. But twelve days from now, you can’t make payroll or pay your office rent.
>
> The problem isn’t that your business isn’t profitable — the problem is a total lack of cash flow visibility."

---

### [0:20 – 0:45] THE PROBLEM
> "Over 82% of small businesses and freelancers fail due to cash flow timing mismatches. 
>
> Today’s accounting software like QuickBooks or Tally only looks *backward* — recording money after it has already left your account. When an important client invoice is overdue by two weeks and collides with non-negotiable rent and salaries, founders discover the cash crunch 48 hours before an overdraft. By then, it’s too late."

---

### [0:45 – 1:15] OUR SOLUTION & LIVE DEMO
> "That is why we built **CashFlow Guardian AI**.
>
> It continuously analyzes income patterns, upcoming liabilities, and uncollected receivables to predict cash shortages 7 to 30 days before they happen.
>
> *(Pointing to screen)*  
> On our dashboard, you don’t just see your current balance of ₹8,500. You see our **Cash Safety Score**, and a clear early warning: **Cash Shortage Predicted around Day 12**.
>
> When you ask *'Why this prediction?'*, our system explains the exact root causes: an overdue ₹28,500 client invoice colliding with ₹37,000 in scheduled payroll and lease payments."

---

### [1:15 – 1:35] INTERACTIVE WHAT-IF SIMULATOR
> "What makes CashFlow Guardian unique is our closed decision loop: **Predict, Explain, Simulate, and Act**.
>
> With our **What-If Stress Simulator**, an operator can test decisions in real-time. For example: *'What if I collect that overdue invoice 5 days earlier?'* 
>
> Instantly, our model recalculates the 30-day forecast. The shortage probability drops from **97.5% down to 18%**, and the safety score jumps back to healthy territory. With one click, the user can trigger an automated payment reminder to make that outcome a reality."

---

### [1:35 – 1:50] TECHNICAL INNOVATION
> "Under the hood, this isn’t hardcoded logic or static charts. We trained a 12-feature Machine Learning ensemble using a **Random Forest Classifier** and **Gradient Boosting Regressors** on 5,000 cash flow scenarios. 
>
> The model achieves **98.1% classification accuracy** and an **ROC-AUC of 0.998**, served via a high-performance FastAPI backend with an inference latency of just 11 milliseconds."

---

### [1:50 – 2:00] IMPACT & STRONG CLOSING
> "CashFlow Guardian AI gives every freelancer and small business owner the predictive foresight of a full-time CFO in their pocket.
>
> **Don’t wait for a cash emergency to discover a shortage. Predict it, prevent it, and protect your business with CashFlow Guardian AI.**
>
> Thank you!"
